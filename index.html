Mutual flourishing for a world without poverty.

Equipping the next generation of changemakers to eliminate global poverty, reduce inequality, and ensure sustainability.

See our work

The Pulte Institute works to build rigorous evidence for what works to eliminate poverty and promote flourishing for people and the planet. Our work is grounded in a steadfast belief in human dignity and the necessity of effective partnerships to generate evidence on successful interventions. The broad expertise of Pulte’s faculty and staff reflects the multi-faceted requirements for human flourishing: adequate nutrition, education, shelter, access to opportunities, a safe and clean environment, and much more. Our work occurs across all of these dimensions/disciplines.

  • Explore Our Research

    We conduct rigorous, data-driven research to identify which global development programs effectively reduce poverty. By building a robust empirical foundation, we provide policymakers and practitioners with the exact evidence needed to scale proven, sustainable interventions.

  • Learn About Our Partnerships

    Addressing complex global challenges requires collective expertise. We actively convene researchers, policymakers, and frontline practitioners to collaborate, share critical insights, and invest in development research across a shifting global landscape.

  • Discover Academic Opportunities

    Grounded in a steadfast belief in human dignity, our research agenda spans multiple disciplines—including education, nutrition, and environmental sustainability. We study the interconnected factors essential to building a more equitable world.

Project Spotlights

Evidence in Action

Explore how Pulte projects investigate solutions to poverty, migration, education, and environmental sustainability—and follow the research behind the work.

Graduating from Learning Poverty

Research in Kenya, Uganda, and Senegal examines how literacy and numeracy interventions can scale sustainably and reach vulnerable learners.

Explore project →

Innovating Green Pathways to Prosperity

This project studies interventions that can reduce poverty while protecting the environment in rural communities across Ghana, India, Kenya, and Peru.

Explore project →

Poverty and Migration

A Central America initiative examines which policies and programs can help people stay and thrive in their home communities.

Explore project →

Forests & Livelihoods (FLARE)

A global network explores how community-managed forests can support biodiversity, livelihoods, and social equity.

Explore project →

Joining Forces to Tackle Global Poverty

Finding lasting solutions to global poverty requires rigorous evidence and a steadfast commitment to human dignity. By integrating the BIG Lab into the Pulte Institute, our researchers are uniting across disciplines to build a stronger engine for development economics—working alongside global partners to test, scale, and deliver interventions that truly work.
